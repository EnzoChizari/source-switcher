Known PCM playback test

For DAC switching, select your browser as master in Source Switcher. Follow this tab from the extension popup, then start one player. Look for the file header format in Sources.

These players generate five-second stereo PCM WAV files at 44.1, 48, 96 and 192 kHz with 16-, 24- and 32-bit samples. Playback starts at low volume.



The extension reads the demo files' WAV headers. The browser may convert the audio before sending it to Windows.
